About

Built with care. Owned by us.

Many good businesses start as someone's side project: an idea built with care, owned by the people who make it and grown on its own merit.

SideQuest Ventures is the home for those ideas. Like the side quests in a game, they run alongside the main story, and they're often where the best rewards are. We start each one small, give it our full attention and grow the ones that prove themselves, one quest at a time.

How we build

Three rules for every quest.
